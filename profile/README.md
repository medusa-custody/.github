# Medusa Custody

Governance-first digital asset custody for institutions and crypto-native companies.

Medusa builds governance-first digital-asset custody for institutions and crypto-native companies. Policy, quorum, and a record around every movement of value.

A wallet answers whether a key can sign. Custody has to answer who may request a movement, who must approve it, which policy applied, whether what was signed matched that approval, and whether someone can reconstruct the path later.

It is the same problem in two rooms.

A bank, a fund, or a corporate treasury needs separation of duties, a preview before anything is signed, and a record that outlasts the people who were in the room.

A crypto-native desk — an exchange, a market maker, a protocol treasury, an OTC firm — needs that same control on Bitcoin and the ledgers it already operates, without a complete key sitting in one place, and without turning every transfer into a ceremony.

Medusa is that control plane, in beta:

- Multi-signature approval, with the quorum the organization actually uses
- Policy that travels with the operation
- A preview of the transaction before signatures are collected
- Threshold signing, so a complete key is not the architecture
- An immutable record of the request, the decision, and the outcome
- Bitcoin and Stellar in the product today, with further ledgers on the roadmap
- Separate workspaces for a desk, a subsidiary, or a client mandate

Medusa is not a licensed custodian and does not hold customer mainnet funds. We are building and validating the path from request, to policy, to approval, to signature, to the ledger.
